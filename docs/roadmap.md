# Roadmap

Each milestone is a separate, reviewable build prompt. Section milestone numbers in the UI come from `packages/core/src/workspaces.ts`.

| # | Milestone | Delivers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Foundation** _(done)_ | Monorepo, core domain rules, app shell, design system, en/ar + RTL, empty role-based workspaces |
| 2 | **Identity, tenancy, audit** _(done)_ | Real auth, organizations, invitations, memberships, roles, Postgres + RLS, audit log |
| 3 | **Platform administration** _(done)_ | Organization directory and profiles, users and memberships, market configuration, integration registry, audit filters |
| 4 | **Developer workspace** _(done)_ | Projects, buildings and phases, units with provenance and history, versioned price lists and payment plans with two-person approval, documents, brokerage project sharing |
| 5 | Rules, sources and connectors | Market rules and fees through approval, email and document-storage connectors, validated spreadsheet imports and authorized feeds (inventory sources), admin MFA, break-glass support access |
| 6 | Brokerage and discovery | Partner inventory view, client registration and protection, viewings, deals, commissions; buyer discovery, shortlist, compare, enquiries, appointments, area profiles |
| 7 | Leads and matching | Unified lead capture, configurable explainable scoring, intent detection, property matching, next-best-action suggestions, SLAs |
| 8 | Conversations and AI assist | WhatsApp, SMS, email and web chat connectors, threaded history, translation, voice notes, consent-aware AI calls, handoff, AI policies |
| 9 | Property management | Portfolio, occupancy, leasing, tenant requests, maintenance and spend approvals, tenant self-service |
| 10 | Attribution and analytics | Campaigns, ad-channel connections, attribution, channel performance, SLA reporting |
