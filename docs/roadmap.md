# Roadmap

Each milestone is a separate, reviewable build prompt. Section milestone numbers in the UI come from `packages/core/src/workspaces.ts`.

| # | Milestone | Delivers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Foundation** _(done)_ | Monorepo, core domain rules, app shell, design system, en/ar + RTL, role-based workspaces |
| 2 | **Identity, tenancy, audit** _(done)_ | Real auth, organizations, invitations, memberships, roles, Postgres + RLS, audit log |
| 3 | **Platform administration** _(done)_ | Organization directory and profiles, users and memberships, market configuration, integration registry, audit filters |
| 4 | **Developer workspace** _(done)_ | Projects, phases, units with provenance, versioned pricing and payment plans with two-person approval, documents, brokerage sharing |
| 5 | **Website and brand** _(done)_ | New logo, dark-and-gold design system, 3D skyline scroll film, marketing pages in English and Arabic, demo requests saved as leads, draft legal pages |
| 6 | **Property management and app restyle** _(done, except approvals)_ | Portfolio, properties and units with recorded occupancy, leases as recorded with tenant contact limited to authorized roles, tenant requests, work orders with recorded vendor confirmation and completion, portfolio summaries from stored records; the app in the dark-and-gold design. Still to come: lease-signing and maintenance-spend approvals (Approvals section) |
| 7 | **Customer experience** _(done)_ | Public discovery of published projects, units and rentals with honest availability; comparison with confirmed, estimated and missing facts marked; enquiries and viewing requests routed by explicit rules; customer support requests; publishing controls and a minimal inbox for organizations |
| 8 | **Project location intelligence** _(done)_ | Location profiles for projects: address, coordinates and area; nearby amenities, planned infrastructure and competing developments, each with distance, source and verification; schematic map and filtered list; explicit sharing with partner brokerages and the public listing |
| 9 | **Authorized inventory sources and imports** _(done)_ | Sources per developer (workspace, partner, file, feed, direct integration) with owner, authorization, platform approval for feeds, health and freshness; staged imports with column mapping, validation, duplicate and conflict detection, preview and confirmation; prices and plans only as drafts for approval; field history with source, batch and actor; partner availability proposals; HTTPS CSV feed adapter |
| 10 | **AI foundation** _(done)_ | Governed AI gateway (Claude via the Anthropic SDK): platform and organization AI policies, off by default; models, monthly budgets with worst-case reservation, autonomy per capability; grounding in approved, sourced, labelled data; personal-data redaction; request log and audit; policy screens. AI features themselves come in later milestones |
| 11 | **Connectors and rules** _(done)_ | Email (SMTP) and document storage (S3-compatible) connectors with real, side-effect-free connection checks and secrets kept server-side; connectors count as ready for the AI gateway only after a recent successful check; market rules and fees through two-person approval with sources and effective dates; two-step sign-in, required for platform staff. WhatsApp, SMS and telephony move to milestone 13; document AI moves to a later milestone |
| 12 | **Leads, matching and brokerage** _(done)_ | Leads captured from enquiries and appointment requests or added with recorded consent; rule-based, explainable scores with staff overrides kept in history; matching to available, non-sample units with approved prices and availability labels; viewings; client registration with developer-set protection periods and hashed duplicate checks; deals that record the approved list price and label at opening; commission claims with two-person approval. AI intent extraction, next-best-action and scoring, VIP routing and launch surge triage move to a later milestone |
| 13 | Conversations and AI calling | Instant replies, follow-up sequences, AI voice agent with consent and handoff, live call assist, tenant assistant, rent reminders, tenant self-service |
| 14 | Insights and discovery | Forecasts, demand insights, attribution, buyer discovery with the home-search assistant |

The AI feature list and its safety rules: [ai-capabilities.md](ai-capabilities.md). Customer discovery and requests: [customer-experience.md](customer-experience.md). Inventory sources and imports: [inventory-sources.md](inventory-sources.md). AI gateway and policies: [ai-gateway.md](ai-gateway.md). Connectors, market rules and MFA: [connectors-and-rules.md](connectors-and-rules.md).
