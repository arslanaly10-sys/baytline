Baytline: three original marks

Each mark is drawn from simple strokes so it holds up as a 16-pixel favicon and on a billboard. Shown in gold on Gulf night (the final scene of the homepage film), in an English and an Arabic lockup, in one colour on sand, and at favicon sizes.

Option A

Threshold

A doorway with the sun rising on its step. It says "a way in to a home" and opens the homepage film at dawn. It's the simplest of the three and the strongest at small sizes.

Option B

Cradle

The Arabic letter ب (baa), the first letter of بيت ("home"), drawn as a bowl holding three rising towers, with its dot as the sun. Bilingual at its core, and the most distinctive of the three.

Option C

Barjeel

A Latin B made of two stacked wind-tower blocks, with the barjeel's vents in the upper bowl. Rooted in Gulf architecture, and it reads clearly as the "B" of Baytline.
